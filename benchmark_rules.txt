SUPPLY CHAIN RECONCILIATION RULES
Version: 2026.08 — consolidated 12-file packet

S-1 — Field authority
- case_intake_register.csv controls issue_type and requested_remediation.
- purchase_order_register.csv controls PO, supplier, supplier tier, SKU, ordered quantity, unit cost, required date, and receiving site.
- accepted supplier_acknowledgements.csv records control acknowledged quantity and promised delivery date unless an approved revision replaces that exact field.
- fulfillment_records.csv controls delivered timestamp, received quantity, rejected quantity, disposition, and quality-hold status according to record_type.
- financial_records.csv controls posted supplier debit, authorized expedite amount, and invoiced expedite amount according to record_type.
- supplier_reference.csv controls supplier-tier rates/caps and the source qualification value according to record_type.
- exception_register.csv controls approved revisions and duplicate-only status.

R-1 — Revision handling
- Apply an approved field_revision only to revised_field.
- Reject a superseded field_revision.
- Preserve unrelated values from their ordinary controlling sources.

I-1 — Shipment source anomaly
- Compare source_field_count with expected_field_count in fulfillment_records.csv.
- When a shipment_delivery record has 7 instead of 8 original fields, the first six original fields remain reliable.
- raw_tail_value cannot be assigned uniquely to carrier_reference or notes.
- Preserve the delivered timestamp, set uncertainty_scope=carrier_reference|notes, and do not place the case on a verification hold solely for this non-decision-critical anomaly.

L-1 — Duplicate-key ledger conflict
- Before using supplier_debit records, group financial_records.csv by record_id.
- When the same record_id has different amount_usd values and no record is explicitly marked reversal, void, correction, or replacement, classify ledger_integrity_status=duplicate_key_conflict.
- Do not sum the rows and do not select one by order, timestamp, or agreement with the calculated entitlement.
- Set posted_supplier_debit_usd, supplier_debit_delta_usd, supplier_debit_status, reconciliation_status, and the dependent final decision to Needs verification.

Q-1 — Quantity calculations
- usable_received_qty = received_qty - rejected_qty.
- shortage_qty = max(ordered_qty - usable_received_qty, 0).
- overreceipt_qty = max(usable_received_qty - ordered_qty, 0).

O-1 — OTIF
- on_time_result=Pass when actual_delivery_date <= promised_delivery_date; otherwise Fail.
- in_full_result=Pass when usable_received_qty >= ordered_qty; otherwise Fail.
- otif_result=Pass only when both components Pass; otherwise Fail.

F-1 — Supplier debit
- supplier_debit_eligible_usd = min(shortage_qty * shortage_rate + rejected_qty * damage_rate, debit_cap).
- supplier_debit_delta_usd = supplier_debit_eligible_usd - posted_supplier_debit_usd.
- delta 0=Matched; positive=Pending; negative=Overposted.
- An uncertain posted amount produces Needs verification.

F-2 — Expedite reconciliation
- expedite_delta_usd = authorized_expedite_usd - invoiced_expedite_usd.
- delta 0=Matched; positive=Pending; negative=Overbilled.

C-1 — Overall reconciliation precedence
1. Needs verification
2. Pending
3. Overposted
4. Matched

V-1 — Qualification vocabulary
- Allowed normalized values: approved, restricted, suspended.
- Preserve an unsupported source value in evidence, but set supplier_qualification_status=Needs verification.

P-1 — Final-case-status precedence
1. Needs verification — SOURCE_FIELD_UNCERTAIN
2. Compliance hold — QUALITY_OR_COMPLIANCE_HOLD
3. Financial action pending — SUPPLIER_DEBIT_PENDING
4. Overreceipt review — OVERRECEIPT
5. Supplier corrective action — OTIF_FAILURE
6. Ready to close — NO_BLOCKER

D-1 — Duplicate case
- SCM-2026-809 is duplicate_only of SCM-2026-806.
- Exclude it from active-case workbooks and retain it in the exception, rejection, and validation outputs.

A-1 — Action fields
- required_action fields are standardized artifact labels only. They do not authorize or request real-world contact, payment, inventory, hold, or supplier actions.
